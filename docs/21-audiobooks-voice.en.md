# 21 · Audiobooks & Voice (Audiobooks & Voice)

> Public-domain audiobooks, voice and narration — usable for podcast material, voice reference and language learning.
> LibriVox, Gutenberg and Musopen are PD / CC0 (cleanest); aggregators like Internet Archive are mixed.
