# 13 · Scans & Photogrammetry (Scans & Photogrammetry)

> Open 3D scans / photogrammetry of museum pieces, artifacts, organisms and terrain — usable directly for education, exhibits, game assets and research.
> Mostly CC0 / public domain; research repos (MorphoSource) often restrict commercial use.
