> **Selection Tips**
> - **Pick colors & schemes** → Coolors (industry benchmark), ColorSpace (full scheme from one color), Adobe Color (with accessibility check)
> - **Recolorable background patterns** → Hero Patterns (CC BY, needs credit), Haikei (no credit)
> - **Wave/shape dividers** → Get Waves, Shape Divider, one-click SVG
> - **Textures** → Fffuel (no credit, no resale), Toptal Subtle Patterns (CC BY, needs credit)
> - ⚠️ **Generator outputs are mostly algorithmic random results with high collision rates**, not suitable as a brand's sole visual identity
> - ⚠️ These tools share a highly consistent clause structure: **outputs are commercial, but bundling as stock/templates for resale is forbidden, as is building a competing service**
