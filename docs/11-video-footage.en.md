# 11 · Open-Source Video Footage（Video Footage）

> Two sections: **11.1 Free Commercial-Use Video Sites** (ready to edit) and **11.2 Institutional & Public-Domain Audiovisual Archives** (the cleanest-licensed historical / space / government footage).
> Video licensing is more fragmented than images — a single site often mixes free, paid, and CC BY-NC clips, so always check each clip's terms before use.

> **Selection Guide**
> - **Most hassle-free**: Pexels, Pixabay, Coverr, ISO Republic — no attribution required; the only ban is bundling into a competing stock site or reselling originals.
> - **Cleanest licensing**: Government works from NASA / NOAA / the National Archives are US public domain; ESA and NASA SVS require source credit.
> - ⚠️ **Institutional archives are "per-item licensed," not "whole-site licensed"**: On Internet Archive and LoC, many items are marked "copyright status unknown," and clips may contain third-party footage, commercial music, or subjects' portraits — always return to the source and read each item's Rights Statement.
> - ⚠️ **"Government work = public domain" holds only in certain jurisdictions**: UK Crown Copyright and many EU government works still usually require attribution under open-government licenses; cross-border use cannot assume the same conclusion.
> - ❌ **British Pathé and AP Archive's educational/preview license ≠ commercial license**; commercial use requires separate payment.
