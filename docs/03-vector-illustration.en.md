> **Selection Tips**
> - **Recolor to match your brand**: unDraw, IRA Design, and Open Doodles all support online direct recoloring, no Figma needed.
> - **Fully attribution-free** → the four CC0 / MIT sites: Open Peeps, Open Doodles, IRA Design, OpenClipart.
> - ⚠️ **Freepik / Vecteezy / Storyset / Icons8** free plans all require attribution, and Freepik's free account has limited downloads; using without attribution is infringement.
> - ⚠️ **VectorPortal forbids redistribution** — you may use the assets in your product, but may not pass the asset pack itself to others.
