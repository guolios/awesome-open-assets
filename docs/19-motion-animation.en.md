> **Selection Tips**
> - **For animated icons without attribution** → UseAnimations (MIT, 298), or use Phosphor/Lucide icons + Lottie rendering
> - **For large volume** → Lordicon (free tier commercial but **must credit**); Icons8, Loading.io likewise need credit
> - **Player choice** → lottie-web for web; ThorVG, rlottie for performance or embedded
> - ⚠️ **LottieFiles is a mixed library**: free and paid mixed; search results show many community uploads with brand logos and cartoon characters that **are not commercial assets** — always check each author's labeled license
> - ❌ **Treat Giphy / Tenor as non-commercial**: platform terms only grant 'in-platform distribution' and don't resolve the film/variety/anime copyright and portrait rights of the original GIFs. For commercial GIFs, use your own assets
