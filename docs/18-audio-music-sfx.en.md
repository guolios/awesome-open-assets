# 18 · Open-Source Audio & Music (Audio, Music & SFX)

> In two sections: **12.1 Free Music & SFX** (scores, ambient sound, sample packs), **12.2 Open Audio Datasets** (audiobooks, speech, environmental sound classification, mostly research licenses).
> Audio is the category with the **highest density of copyright traps** in the whole repo: a single piece of music carries three layers of rights — composition, recording, and performance — holding one does not mean you hold them all.

**Legend**: ✅ Commercial use free ｜ ⚠️ Partially commercial ｜ ❌ Non-commercial only　·　Attribution: Not required / Required / Varies

> **Selection Tips**
> - **For self-media scoring** → Pixabay Music, Mixkit Music (no attribution); YouTube Audio Library, check per-track attribution
> - **For game SFX** → Freesound (**filter out NC by license**), ZapSplat (must credit), SampleFocus (no attribution but AI training banned)
> - **Classical** → Musopen's scores and recordings are public domain, but free accounts have download quotas
> - **Audiobooks / TTS training** → LibriVox is public domain, Common Voice is CC0; these two are cleanest
> - ❌ **Most 12.2 datasets are licensed for research only**: ESC-50 and UrbanSound8K are explicitly NC-SA; using them to train commercial models goes beyond the license
> - ⚠️ **NCS (NoCopyrightSounds) is not public domain** — it is copyrighted music, free for creators to use, but explicitly forbids repackaging as a stock library
