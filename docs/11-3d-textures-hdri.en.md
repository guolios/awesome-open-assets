> **Selection Guide**
> - **HDRI environment lighting** → Poly Haven is the only recommendation; quality and resolution surpass industry standards
> - **PBR materials (metal/wood/stone)** → ambientCG, with full 2K/4K/8K resolutions and complete normal, roughness, AO maps
> - **Low-poly scenes** → Quaternius and Kay Lousberg bundle entire CC0 asset packs, great for quick small games or demos
> - ⚠️ **Always filter by license before downloading from Sketchfab**: many models are CC BY-NC (non-commercial); mistakes bring legal risk
> - Note: the old CC0 Textures (cc0textures.com) was renamed to **ambientCG** in 2021; no need to revisit old domains in tutorials
