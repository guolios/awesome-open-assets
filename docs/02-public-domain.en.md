# 02 · Public Domain & Institutional Collections (Public Domain & Cultural Heritage)

> Scanned collections opened by museums, libraries, and space agencies, **mostly CC0 or public domain — the cleanest-licensed tier of assets**.
> Ideal for book covers, cultural designs, historical research, wallpapers, and any commercial use where you want to fully avoid copyright risk.

> **Selection Tips**
> - For **book covers / cultural merchandise**: prioritize The Met, Rijksmuseum, Smithsonian — the artworks themselves are long in the public domain, and the scans are CC0, double safety.
> - For **tech / space themes**: NASA Image Library is fully public domain, even astronaut portraits are commercial-usable.
> - ⚠️ **Always check mixed-license platforms item by item**: within Wikimedia Commons, Europeana, and DPLA, different entries on the same site may have entirely different licenses; NYPL has many recent photos still under copyright.
