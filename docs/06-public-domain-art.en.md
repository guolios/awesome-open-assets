# 06 · Public Domain Art (Public Domain Art)

> Public-domain / CC0 art and vintage illustrations opened by museums, libraries and archives — a goldmine for posters, covers and heritage design.
> Most are CC0 or PD (commercial OK, no attribution); a few need per-item rights checks.

> Cleanest licenses → CC0: Met Museum, Rijksmuseum, Smithsonian, Paris Musées, Art Institute of Chicago.
> Wikimedia Commons / Europeana are aggregators with mixed licenses — open each item's license before use.
> For mass merchandising of famous artworks, some museums add extra terms on derivatives.
