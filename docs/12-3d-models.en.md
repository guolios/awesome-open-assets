# 12 · 3D Models (3D Models)

> Standalone 3D model repositories — distinct from 11 (textures/HDRI): these are importable models for modeling, games and rendering.
> Poly Pizza, Quaternius and Kenney are fully CC0; aggregators like Sketchfab let you filter by license.
