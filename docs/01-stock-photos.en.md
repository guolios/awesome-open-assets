> **Selection Tips**
> - Want the **cleanest licensing** → look for CC0: StockSnap, Negative Space, Pixnio, Life of Pix, LibreShot, Skitterphoto, Freestocks, Picography
> - Want the **largest volume** → Pexels, Pixabay, Picjumbo
> - **Shared red lines of Unsplash / Pexels / Pixabay**: no reselling as-is (posters, mugs), no repackaging into a competing stock library
